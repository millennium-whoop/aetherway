The missiles raced toward him.

His wings ignited, unfurling behind him in a blazing halo.

Sparks and embers whirled through the air.

KLINK!!

It spun violently away—

BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!!!
