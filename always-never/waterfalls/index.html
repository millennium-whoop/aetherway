Cleansing

Rocky terrain gives way to patches of green grass, all washed in a bluish tint. Fire press the cold stone and damp earth beneath his bare feet.

Ahead, a towering waterfall roars, the crash of water rising into the air and lifting a veil of fog.

He lifts his arms and looks himself over. Pajamas? He tugs at the loose sleeves, smoothing the fabric until it sits right. A slow yawn follows, leaving his eyes half-lidded.

He walks a little further. Behind the water curtain, dozens of great dusky swifts cling vertically to the rock face. On scythe-shaped wings they dart through the spray, high-pitched calls ringing out as they catch insects in midair.

The space around him is enclosed by stone—jagged rocks rising close on all sides, their surfaces thick with moss. Fire tilts his head upward. Through the opening between the rocks, he can see the blue sky, clouds sliding slowly across it.

He closes his eyes and lets the gentle breeze brush through his hair.

Opening them, he sees her in the distance. The familiar line of her back, the Japanese sailor school uniform, the long braid falling neatly behind her as she sits on the cliff’s edge.

He walks toward her until the ground thins beneath him. He drops low and crawls to the edge, glancing down at the immense drop—the waterfalls plunging through open air, shrinking as they feed a distant river.

Fire stops behind her, hesitates, then sits, folding in on himself, arms wrapped around his knees.

Lady Thread sits with her feet dangling freely over the abyss, gazing into a small hand mirror, its surface dotted with fine droplets of mist. As she turns it just so, her reflection slips away, replaced by a pale flash of sky drifting across the glass.

She tilts her head left, then right, testing the light, gives herself an exaggerated wink, and poses with two fingers in a soft V near her cheek. She puckers her lips into a tiny kiss, rotates the mirror, leans closer, then pulls it away again—puffing her cheeks and pressing them gently with her fingertips, before finally practicing an innocent smile and letting it slip into a mischievous smirk.

“Oh, loved that one!” he says, letting it slip in his excitement.

「u really think so〜？」 she asks, eyes closed, preening slightly, absorbed in the mirror. Then she tilts the mirror just enough to catch his face reflected in the background.

「kyaaah〜！ i didn’t see u there ne〜！」 she screams suddenly.

Fire jumps. She puffs her cheeks, then bursts out laughing.

She stands up, stepping backward along the cliff path.

「ah！！ yabai！！ this is super off limits ４ u！！」 she exclaims, pressing the mirror against her chest to block its reflection.

Fire stands as well, slowly, carefully moving a step toward her. He scratches the back of his neck and averts his gaze, cheeks warming.

“Huh? I thought it was cute,” he says, voice soft, eyes darting between her and the mirror.

「of course it is cute！！ that is not what i mean〜！！」 she insists. 「listen ne〜 this mirror is dangerous ４ u！！」

Fire freezes, eyes narrowing, his lips pressing into a tight line. “…For me?”

「not everyone is prepared ２ see it！！」 she says, tilting the mirror slightly.

“Huh?! I’m not prepared?!” His voice rises sharply. He stomps a foot, hands clenching at his sides, and his shoulders tense. His face flushes, lips trembling slightly.

「it shows who u truly are！！」 she says, voice sharp.

“Give me this thing!” He steps forward, hand outstretched.

She places the mirror against her back, and walks backward slowly, keeping her eyes on him. 「ah〜 see！？ this response・・・ it tells me everything！！」

Her expression shifts suddenly—she frowns, gaze locking onto his eyes, serious for the first time.

The moment stretches.

Fire finally exhales, taking a deep breath. He runs a hand through his hair, glances down, then to the side.

“C’mon…" he says, softer now “I can handle a mirror.”

She watches him for a long moment, unreadable. A breeze slips between them, brushing her skirt, grazing his sleeves, carrying the scent of moss and stone.

Behind the curtain of water, the swifts cry out sharply—then fall silent again.

「well〜 if u really want ２・・・and u think ur ready・・・i don’t have a choice ne〜 besides・・・」 She glances up with a distracted little grin. 「nobody is ever ready anyway〜 fufu〜」 She chuckles.

She stretches her arm just slightly, the mirror facing down. 「then it is decided・・・」

Suddenly she snaps it back. 「with one condition！！」

“Sure,” Fire mutters.

「u have ２ tell me which expression was ur fave〜」

He drops a hand onto his head and squeezes his eyes shut. “Huuuuuh…”

Then, without opening them, he sticks out his tongue and scrunches his face. “The one like this.”

「eh！？！」 She laughs. 「i did not do that one！！」

“Let me see, okay?”

「okay〜」 She stretches her arm again. 「here it is〜」

「stop stop〜 one more tiny thing！！」 she exclaims, pulling the mirror back quickly.

“Whaaaaat?”

「after u see it・・・・・・u cannot unsee it！！ i am warning u properly ne〜！！」

Fire’s throat tightens. He freezes, right hand suspended in midair. Inches away.

He looks down. Closes his eyes. Takes a deep breath and holds it. Then he opens them, decisively.

His heart pounds. A cloud of mist drifts by on the soft wind, partially hiding her from view.

Suddenly, the clouds split. Sunlight pours through, striking droplets in her moving braid, sparkling in the sudden sun.

The wind stops. The roar of the waterfall fades, the swifts’ cries vanish, even the distant river seems to hold its breath.

She stands there in radiant clarity. The grin on her lips is satisfied, but her eyes betray a quiet melancholy.

He exhales. Then, slowly, reaches for the mirror.
