Sprites It should work with any sprites, really, but I especially love how GBC sprites look with this technique! Gaps The grid, or gaps between pixels are fully transparent perfectly blending with shirt’s colors! It’s like your chest is a LCD matrix display! Want something subtler? Increase the gap between pixels for a sleek, more minimalist effect. Custom Shapes! Pixels can be shaped like squares, circles, hearts, sparkles, crosses, stars or any custom shape! Portraits and Cutscenes It’s perfect for character portraits and cutscenes too! I’m especially loving how the designs from Pokemon Pinball , Pinball RS , and Pokemon Card GB2 turned out! Photos It works with photos too! Turn your pet or beloved idol into a cool, retro, unique, pixelated, wearable masterpiece! (Make sure the image is zoomed in for the best results!)
