About

Project

The Aetherway is a sort of spin-off of my original species/adopts project, the Dimensional Ladies (2018–present). The concept has existed since then as the world they inhabit—but it wasn’t until 2024 that I decided to begin writing stories set in it.

Writing is relatively new to me. I realized that cool designs alone weren’t enough to carry my brand; my characters needed stories, something that could genuinely resonate with people. So I started writing down all the wild ideas that keep me up at night—and in doing so, I discovered a new side of myself as a writer. It’s been fulfilling and healing. After years of relying solely on commissions, this has become a creative space that truly feels like my own.

The idea for this website grew out of a frustration with the current state of the internet, social media, and The Algorithm. It’s part of a broader movement to restore some of the spirit of the late ’90s and early 2000s—when online spaces prioritized personal expression over social validation, and every website carried its creator’s unique personality.
