About

Project

The Aetherway is a spin-off of my original species/adopts project, Dimensional Ladies (2018–present). What started as the world those characters inhabited eventually evolved into a storytelling project of its own in 2024.

As I began jotting down all the wild ideas that keep me up at night, I discovered a new side of myself as a writer. The process has been both fulfilling and healing. After years of relying solely on art commissions, The Aetherway has become a creative space that feels entirely my own.

Author

Greetings, this is corbari_j — carbon-based, warm-blooded, proudly mammalian. 35 cycles deep, broadcasting from São Paulo, Brazil.

Somehow became the antisocial priest of a certain Goddess of Connection.

I’m currently on a neverending mission to draw every OC ever conceived. Your support helps me get closer to my dream: drawing only my OCs full-time. Thank you!

Aetherway is independent. Your support helps.
