Lady Spiral

Her presence is often felt in moments when everything begins to spiral out of control. Whether she is the cause of the chaos or simply a witness to it remains a mystery.

Lady Herself

Lady Nobody admired Lady Somebody and wished to become like her. She used the Dreamer’s Mirror to see herself as Lady Somebody, but instead of a reflection, the mirror showed nothing. Overwhelmed by this emptiness, Lady Nobody finally accepted her own identity. As she did, the mirror began to shine brightly. When the light faded, it revealed Lady Herself, her true self. This moment marked her acceptance of who she truly was.

Lady Entanglement

She weaves the invisible threads between all things, connecting them in ways that defy distance or time, tying together people, places and moments. The worship of Lady Entanglement is typically done on a ceremony where people share a meal of spaghetti or ramen together, sharing experiences about how their lives have intertwined with others.

Labeland

Meanwhile, in Some Dimension…

Navigating through the green hills, I’ve arrived at this place where giant label makers were placed. People would approach them, get a label on themselves, and proceed to the next label maker. The process would repeat endlessly. Some had their bodies completely covered.

On top of the hill, a figure without a single label observed the scene with a happy face. I sat by his side and asked, “What’s happening here?”
