Ant Hill

Meanwhile, in This Dimension…

After school, on her way home, Zero crouched by an ant hill, watching as the tiny workers tirelessly carried pebbles out of their tunnels, each moving with purpose. A few feet away, she noticed hundreds of ants swarming around a lollipop, likely dropped by some careless kid earlier.

“They’re fascinating,” she murmured to herself.

She gently reached into a nearby puddle, trying to rescue an ant struggling to stay afloat.

About

Project

The Aetherway is a spin-off of my original species/adopts project, Dimensional Ladies (2018–present). The concept began as the world they inhabit, but it wasn’t until 2024 that I started writing stories set within it.

As I began jotting down all the wild ideas that keep me up at night, I discovered a new side of myself as a writer. The process has been both fulfilling and healing. After years of relying solely on art commissions, The Aetherway has become a creative space that feels entirely my own.
